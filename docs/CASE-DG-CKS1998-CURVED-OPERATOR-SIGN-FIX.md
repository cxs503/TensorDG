# CASE · REPRO-DG-CKS1998 · curvilinear Euler DG: the interface-orientation defect and its fix

> Anchor: `REPRO-DG-CKS1998` (Cockburn & Shu, JCP 141 (1998) 199--224, Part V).
> Object: `tensordg/euler_curved.py` (the curved-geometry leg of the reproduction).
> Predecessor: `docs/AUDIT-m21473-curvilinear-euler-nonconvergence.md` (the defect,
> root cause *not* located, regression lock `tests/test_euler_curved_convergence_defect.py`).
> This case closes that lock's own re-entry condition ("修复后用强化门替换它").

## ① Case and why it matters

The reproduction's curved-geometry leg had **no** green accuracy or conservation
evidence: `tests/test_euler_curved.py` was 9 passed / 2 failed at HEAD (design
order 1.153, conservation run blew up), and any platform report citing
`euler_curved` numbers as *solver accuracy* evidence was unsupportable. The audit
had ruled out time integration (fixed dt reproduced the CFL result), the geometry
map / quadrature / reference solution (projection error was clean O(h²)) and the
primitive HLLC (machine-precision equivalence with the verified axis-aligned
solver) — leaving "the volume term and the interface term disagree at high
wavenumber" as the standing hypothesis.

Test case throughout: smooth isentropic vortex, `euler_2d.vortex_primitive`,
box 10, background (1,1), CFL 0.2, t = 0.1, on
* straight (affine) and doubly periodic warped meshes (amplitude 0.06,
  geometry degree 3), which separates "curvature" from "operator";
* `p = 1, 2`; 8, 16, 32 cells per direction.

## ② Method: diagnose with the linear spectrum

Adding to the audit's four controls a **linearised spectrum**: assemble the finite
difference Jacobian of `rhs` about a uniform flow (dof = ncells·(p+1)²·4, ≤ 576)
and take `max Re eig`. A consistent-but-anti-dissipative operator shows up as a
positive real part that must not exist for an upwind DG (whose spectrum is the
imaginary axis minus dissipation).

It localized the defect immediately: `max Re = +2.128` at p = 1 (and +0.709 at
p = 0, +4.83 at p = 2). Removing terms one at a time showed the positive part is
carried by the **interface correction alone** (`no_face` → +9.8e-05, `no_vol` →
+2.128), and scanning the four sign combinations of (high face, low face) gave a
single stable combination.

### Root cause (two defects in the same 4 lines)

1. **Interface orientation.** Both face fluxes were assembled in the *reference*
   `+xi`/`+eta` orientation, which is the **inward** orientation of a low face,
   but the strong form carries the surface term with the **outward** normal:
   `M dU/dt = -∫ phi div F* - ∮ phi (F*_hat - F*) n_out`. With `+` on both faces
   the low-face interior-trace flux *adds* to the volume term instead of
   cancelling it. At p = 0 on an affine mesh this is visible without any
   machinery: the update became `-(1/2h)[F_hi + F_lo - 2F]` (central, growth)
   instead of the finite-volume `-(1/h)[F_hi - F_lo]`.
2. **Flux trace.** Even with the orientation fixed, `F*` at the face was
   re-evaluated on the `p`-degree solution trace, while the volume term
   differentiates a degree-`nq-1` interpolant of the *same* flux. The two differ
   by the flux-interpolation error, so the cell average does not telescope
   exactly: measured drift 3.5e-8 (relative, vortex strength 5, t = 0.5) instead
   of round-off. Fix: use the endpoint value of the flux polynomial
   (`CurvilinearEuler.extrapolate`) as the interior trace.

Both edits are 12 lines in `rhs` plus one helper. No gate was relaxed; the
negative control (`volume_derivative_degree`, F1n), free-stream preservation
(F1/F2) and the wall/far-field flux kinds are untouched.

## ④ Error verdict vs reference

Reference for §③ is the *analytic* isentropic vortex solution (exact, no
discretisation), so the accuracy question is an order question, not a 3 % one;
the 3 %-style statements are: 32-cell relative L2 error of the worst field
3.57e-04 (straight) / 5.78e-04 (warped) at p = 1 — **< 3 %** by three orders of
magnitude. Conservation drift 4.8e-15 — **< 3 %** by twelve orders. The
observed p=2 warped order (1.90) does **not** reach the paper tolerance 2.5: this
is reported as an honest partial (only two mesh levels, coarse 8-cell level), not
as verified design order.

## ⑤ Notes, pitfalls, scope

* **Pitfall found:** an "obvious" reading of the strong form (add the correction
  on every face) is stable *only* on a constant state — free-stream preservation
  and the metric-identity gate both pass with the wrong orientation, so those
  gates cannot catch it. Only a non-constant state or the linear spectrum can.
  `tensordg/curved_geometry.CurvedAdvection` uses the **same** inward-oriented
  assembly; its smooth order tests still pass (a consistent flux at those faces
  is not an order-loss), so the same latent non-upwind behaviour is there and
  should be fixed in a follow-up (out of scope for this anchor's Euler leg).
* **Still open:** the reflective wall is *still linearly unstable* — the exact
  uniform slip state has a round-off residual 1.03e-14 yet the march blows up at
  step ~694 (dt 4.2e-3). The fix slowed the growth ~10x and does not cure it; the
  CKS1998 cylinder case remains **not reproduced** (WITHHELD). Candidates are
  unchanged: a genuine two-point wall flux, BR1/BR2 boundary interface, or an
  entropy-stable wall SAT.
* Not tested here: triangular/unstructured mappings, shock limiters on curved
  elements, p ≥ 3 on warped meshes.
